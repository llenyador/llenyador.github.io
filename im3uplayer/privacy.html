Privacy Policy

What iM3U Cloud Player ("the app") handles on iPhone, iPad and Mac, what stays on your devices and in your iCloud, what leaves them, and the choices you have.

No sign-up Playlists in your iCloud Nothing sold

The short version

The app has no account of its own and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your playlists, lists, favorites and recents are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot read. Channels and TV guides are downloaded directly from the links you add. Google Firebase receives usage statistics and crash reports so we can improve the app. If the app shows ads, Google serves them, with consent where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

iM3U Cloud Player is developed by Aitor Salvador ("we", "us"), the controller of the information described here. Contact: llenyador.io@gmail.com.

Your iCloud and your device

  • Saved in your private iCloud (CloudKit): the playlists you add (name, image and link), your custom lists, favorites and recents. This lets them sync across your devices. It lives in your iCloud account's private database: Apple stores it under your account and we have no access to it.
  • Stored only on your device: the downloaded and parsed playlists, TV guide data, channel images and settings, kept as a cache so the app opens quickly. Settings › Clear cache deletes it.
  • Your iCloud identifier: the app reads the anonymous iCloud user record ID that Apple gives each app. It is not your Apple Account email or name.

Playlist links can include the username and password your provider gave you. They stay in your iCloud and on your devices; we never receive them.

3

Information that leaves your device

Playlists, streams and TV guides

When you add or refresh a playlist, play a channel or load a TV guide, the app connects directly to the servers in the links you added. Those servers, run by your provider, not by us, see your IP address and the request, as with any web request. Their privacy practices are their own.

Usage statistics

The app sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics: screens viewed, buttons used, playlists and lists created, channels played, saved or cast, search terms, purchase steps and errors, with a Firebase app-instance ID. Some events include the name of the channel, list or playlist involved, and the text you searched. We use this to understand which features are used and to fix problems. We do not link it to your name or email.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a report: the stack trace or error, device model, OS version, app version and a Crashlytics installation identifier. We use it only to fix bugs.

Remote configuration

The app downloads settings from Google Firebase Remote Config (for example, which features are on). The request includes a Firebase installation identifier.

Purchases (PRO)

PRO subscriptions are bought through the App Store. RevenueCat manages them for us using your anonymous iCloud user record ID as the app user ID, store transaction IDs and the products you own, so we can unlock and restore PRO on your devices. We never receive your payment details.

Ads

When ads are shown in the free version, they are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Before ads load, Google's consent form is shown where the law requires it, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy choices. On iOS the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); only if you allow it does Google receive the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. Google may also process your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics. PRO subscribers don't see ads.

Chromecast

To find Chromecast devices the app searches your local network. When you cast, the stream link is sent to your Chromecast, which then plays it directly from your provider. Nothing about this is sent to us.

How long we keep it

  • Data in your iCloud stays until you delete it in the app or remove the app's data from iCloud.
  • The cache on your device stays until you clear it or delete the app.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data follow Google's retention settings (Analytics: 14 months at most; Crashlytics: 90 days).
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to deliver and restore PRO and to meet accounting and legal obligations.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to answer you.
7

Your choices and rights

  • Review your ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices (where available), or subscribe to PRO to remove ads.
  • Allow or deny tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device.
  • Delete playlists and lists in the app; they are removed from your iCloud too. You can also delete all of the app's iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
  • Use Settings › Clear cache, or delete the app, to remove the data stored on your device.
  • Deny Local Network access if you don't use Chromecast.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details that let us find the data in question. Write to llenyador.io@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

8

Children

iM3U Cloud Player is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9

Security

Data the app sends to us and our providers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and iCloud data is protected by Apple. Streams and playlists you add are loaded from the addresses you choose; if a link uses plain http, that connection is not encrypted.

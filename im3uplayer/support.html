Playlists

What do I need to start?

An M3U playlist link, usually given to you by your IPTV provider or one you create yourself. iM3U Cloud Player is a player only: it does not provide, sell or include any channels or media content.

How do I add a playlist?
  1. On the home screen, tap Add playlist.
  2. Give it a name and paste the playlist link (it usually ends in .m3u or .m3u8, or contains type=m3u).
  3. Accept the content disclaimer and tap Create list.
"URL is not a valid M3U file" or "Could not get contents of url".
  • Open the link in Safari: it should download or show a text file that starts with #EXTM3U. If it shows a web page or an error, the link is wrong or has expired.
  • Many providers' links expire or are limited to a number of devices. Ask your provider for a fresh link.
  • Check your connection and try again.
How do I refresh or edit a playlist?

On the home screen, tap Edit and choose the playlist to change its name, image or link. Saving it downloads the playlist again. To free space, use Settings › Clear cache; your playlists stay in iCloud.

Playback, TV guide and Chromecast

A channel doesn't play or keeps buffering.
  • Channels are streamed directly from your provider's servers, so most playback problems come from the stream itself. Try another channel from the same playlist; if none play, contact your provider.
  • Try a faster or different network (Wi-Fi instead of mobile data, or the other way round).
  • Close the player and open the channel again.
Why is there no TV guide for my channels?

The guide (EPG) comes from your playlist. If the playlist includes a guide link (url-tvg or x-tvg-url in its first line), the app downloads it and shows what's on now and next. If you see "This list doesn't include a TV guide", ask your provider for a playlist with EPG.

How do I cast to my TV?

Your iPhone or iPad and your Chromecast or Google TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Tap the cast button, choose your device and start a channel. If no device appears, allow Local Network access for the app in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network, then restart the app.

Does it support Picture in Picture?

Yes. While a channel is playing, tap the Picture in Picture button to keep watching in a floating window while you use other apps.

iCloud, lists and devices

"An iCloud account is required to continue".

The app has no sign-up of its own: it stores your playlists, lists, favorites and recents in your own iCloud. If you are signed in and still see this message:

  1. Open Settings › [your name] › iCloud and make sure iCloud Drive is on.
  2. If it still fails, sign out of iCloud and sign in again, then reopen the app.
  3. Check that you have a working internet connection the first time you open the app.
How do I get my playlists on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account on the other iPhone, iPad or Mac and open the app. Your playlists, favorites, recents and custom lists sync through iCloud.

How do I save channels to a list?

Tap the save button on a channel's row and pick Favorites or one of your custom lists. Channels you watch appear in Recents automatically.

PRO and purchases

What does PRO include?

The free version has every player feature. PRO removes ads and lets you create unlimited custom lists with unlimited items (the free version allows 1 custom list with up to 5 items).

How do I restore my PRO subscription?

Open Settings › Upgrade to PRO and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

PRO renews automatically until you cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date from Settings › Manage your subscriptions in the app, or from your Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Content and privacy

Do you provide channels or playlists?

No. iM3U Cloud Player does not provide or include any media content and is not affiliated with any content provider. You are responsible for the playlists you add, and we do not endorse streaming content you are not authorized to watch.

What data does the app collect?

Your playlists live in your own private iCloud, which we can't read. The app uses Google Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

What Murder Sudoku: Whodoku ("Whodoku", "the app") handles on iOS and Android, what stays on your device, what leaves it, and the choices you have.

No account Statistics optional Nothing sold

The short version

Whodoku has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress lives on your device. Anonymous game statistics are sent only while Share anonymous statistics is on, and you can turn it off at any time. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google, with consent where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Whodoku is developed by Aitor Salvador ("we", "us"), the controller of the information described here. Contact: llenyador.io@gmail.com.

2

Information that stays on your device

  • Game progress: solved cases, stars, coins, hints, achievements, login calendar, tutorial progress and saved boards.
  • Settings: your statistics choice, the daily reminder and similar preferences.
  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on, the reminder is a local notification scheduled by your device. No push service is used.
  • Beta play log (testers only, off by default): if you turn it on, the app records your moves and feedback in a local file. The app never sends it; you decide whether to share it through your device's share sheet. Turning it off deletes it.

This data is not sent to us. Like other app data, it can be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google backup), which Apple or Google keep under your own account.

3

Information that leaves your device

Anonymous game statistics (optional)

While Share anonymous statistics is on, the app sends:

  • One gameplay report per solved case to our own server: the case, its difficulty and board size, the UTC day, active play time, stars, failed checks, hints used, undos, platform and app version, plus a random player ID created by the app. It contains no name, account, device or advertising identifier, and no free text.
  • Usage events to Google Firebase Analytics: screens viewed, cases started, solved or left, hints, coins earned, achievements and Store or ad steps, with a Firebase app-instance ID. We do not set a user ID and do not send advertising IDs to Analytics. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are kept denied for Analytics.

We use this to balance puzzle difficulty, find confusing steps and improve the game. The random player ID only groups reports from the same install so we can see how difficulty changes over time; it is not linked to who you are.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the switch starts off and nothing is sent unless you turn it on. Elsewhere it starts on. You can change it in the first-launch privacy step or later in Profile. Turning it off stops collection, clears data queued on the device and resets the Firebase Analytics data on the device.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report: the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and a Crashlytics installation identifier. It contains no account or gameplay data. We use it only to fix bugs.

Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and Premium are bought through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat manages them for us using an anonymous app user ID, store transaction IDs and the products you own, so we can deliver and restore your purchases. We never receive your payment details.

Rewarded ads (optional)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward in the Store. They are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Before an ad loads, Google's consent form is shown where the law requires it, and no ad is requested without the consent it needs. Google may process device and advertising identifiers (on Android the advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions and diagnostics. On iOS the app does not ask to track you and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Downloading new cases

The app may download the daily case catalog. That request carries no identifiers; like any web request, the server sees your IP address.

How long we keep it

  • Gameplay reports are deleted automatically after 365 days.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data follow Google's retention settings (Analytics: 14 months at most; Crashlytics: 90 days).
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to deliver and restore purchases and to meet accounting and legal obligations.
  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app (or its backup).
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to answer you.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn Share anonymous statistics off in Profile.
  • Review your ad consent from the consent form shown before rewarded ads, or skip ads altogether: no part of the game requires watching one.
  • Turn the daily reminder off in Profile or in your device's notification settings.
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete the app to remove the data stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details that let us find the data in question. Write to llenyador.io@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

8

Children

Whodoku is a mystery puzzle game for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9

Security

All data the app sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our server accepts only anonymous reports and is not readable by apps or the public.

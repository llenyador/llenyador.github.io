Playing

How does a case work?

Each case is a floor plan split into rooms, with a victim and a few suspects. Every suspect gives one clue. Place each person on the plan so that every clue is true. The killer is the person left alone in a room with the victim. Every case has exactly one solution you can reach by deduction alone, without guessing.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Use a hint: hints point you to the next deduction you can make. Hints cost coins, which you earn by solving cases, from daily login rewards, or from optional rewarded ads.

How do I unlock more cases?

New cases unlock with stars. Every solved case earns at least one star, so the campaign never needs coins, purchases or ads.

What is the Daily case?

A new case every day. You can turn on a daily reminder in Profile to get one notification when it opens.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store or Profile and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Premium and the Starter Pack are restored this way. Coins you already spent are not.

I paid but didn't get my coins or Premium.
  • Check your connection, then close and reopen the app.
  • Tap Restore purchases in the Store.
  • If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from your store receipt (never send card details).
What does Premium include?

Premium doubles your daily login rewards, for good. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid. On iOS, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund help.

Ads, progress and devices

Does the game show ads?

Only if you ask for one. In the Store you can watch a short video to earn coins, a few times a day. There are no banners and no ads between cases.

It says "No ad available right now."

Ads aren't always available, especially offline or after you declined ad consent. Try again later; nothing else in the game depends on ads.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

Your progress is stored on your device and has no account. It moves to a new device when you restore it from an iCloud or Google backup of the old one. Deleting the app without a backup deletes your progress.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Cases, hints and progress all work offline. The Store, ads and new Daily cases need a connection.

Privacy

What data does the app collect?

No account and no personal details. Anonymous game statistics are sent only while Share anonymous statistics is on, and you can turn it off in Profile. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
